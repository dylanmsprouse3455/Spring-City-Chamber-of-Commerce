Dining Tips

Check hours before going

Small-town dining hours can shift by season, staffing, and events. Call ahead when possible.

Use food as the middle stop

Good visitor flow: attraction or event first, food second, then shopping or lodging after.

Support local businesses

Food pages should naturally send traffic toward Chamber members and local restaurants.

Keep planning your visit

Dining works best when connected to the full visit: where to stay, what to do, what events are happening, and which businesses are Chamber members.
